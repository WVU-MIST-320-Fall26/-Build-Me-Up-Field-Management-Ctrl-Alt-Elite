## Platform Foundation
**Label:** `epic:platform-foundation`

The core cloud infrastructure, security, and project setup the rest of the portal depends on. Covers environments and CI/CD, role-based access control for field and office users, and the module PMs use to create projects, budgets, and team rosters.

## Field Mobile Experience
**Label:** `epic:field-mobile`

Tools that let job-site field engineers capture information from a phone or tablet, even without a signal. Includes daily site logs with photos and progress, offline sync, and a blueprint viewer for flagging modifications against the current drawing set.

## Change Management
**Label:** `epic:change-management`

The end-to-end change order process: field teams submit change requests, project managers review and approve them, and approved changes update the project budget in real time. This epic addresses the core business pain of untracked change orders.

## Safety
**Label:** `epic:safety`

Digital reporting of safety incidents and near-misses with immediate alerts, plus tracking of corrective actions through to closure. Replaces paper safety logs and supports OSHA recordkeeping.

## Visibility & Reporting
**Label:** `epic:reporting`

Gives project managers and leadership real-time insight into project health. Includes the cost-vs-progress dashboard, notifications and activity feeds, global search across all records, and exportable reports for owners and executives.

## Integration & Compliance
**Label:** `epic:integration-compliance`

Connects the portal to the company's accounting/ERP system so costs aren't re-keyed, and provides an immutable audit trail and data retention rules for dispute resolution and legal compliance.
