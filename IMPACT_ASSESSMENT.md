# CCB Impact Assessment: Build Me Up Field Management

## 1. Change Request Summary
- **Stakeholder:** Regional Safety & Risk Director
- **Request:** Integrate smart wearables to enforce automated crane geo-fencing, trigger site sirens, lock out machinery, and auto-log OSHA violations.

## 2. Project Baseline Impact Assessment
- **Scope Baseline:** Major scope creep; expands boundaries beyond the approved field portal deliverables into physical hardware and machinery.
- **Schedule Baseline:**  Hardware testing and safety sign-offs will delay Sprint 3 execution and may make us miss the November 20, 2026  milestone.
- **Cost & Resource Allocation:**  Requires specialized embedded systems and hardware testing.
- **Risk & Quality Management:** Legal and liability risks tied to automated machinery shutoffs and direct, unverified (OSHA) reporting.

## 3. Team Recommendation & Action
- **Decision:** **REJECT** (or **DEFER** to a Phase 2 product release)
- **Justification:** Breaches scope, schedule, and costs without real contingency reserves before Sprint 3 planning.
