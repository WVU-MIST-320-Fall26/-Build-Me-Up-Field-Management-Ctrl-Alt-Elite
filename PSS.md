# Project Scope Statement
## Overview
__Name__: Build Me Up Field Management Project

__Description__: Our goal is to create a field operations & chain management portal by December 4th, 2026. This portal would allow field teams to submit site logs, safety incidents, blueprint modifications, and change requests without the issue of disorganization. The portal will also allow project managers to review, approve, and link chain orders to budget impacts with ease. The project will be completed by a team of four and has a budget of $560,000.

## Project Deliverables
**Deliverable 1:** Mobile-accessible daily site logs and safety incident tracking module.

**Deliverable 2:** Real-time blueprint access portal with mobile markup tools.

**Deliverable 3:** Digital change request submission system for field teams.

**Deliverable 4:** Centralized dashboard for PMs to review, approve, and link change orders to the project budget.

## Acceptance Criteria
* The system must reduce change order approval turnaround time to under 24 hours.
* The system must successfully link submitted change orders to real-time project budget impacts.
* Site logs, safety reports, and blueprints must be fully accessible and editable via mobile devices in the field.
